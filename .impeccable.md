## Design Context

### Users
Arabic-speaking academy administrators and teachers who work in RTL dashboards. Their primary job is to find the right group of students, understand their learning activity, and export operational reports quickly without composing technical filters.

### Brand Personality
Clear, dependable, and practical. The interface should create confidence and calm, with direct Arabic labels and visible state changes.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined, light, utilitarian dashboard that follows the existing Massar Academy navy palette and semantic design tokens. Prefer clear grouping, restrained color, strong borders where they clarify ownership, and progressive disclosure. Avoid decorative effects, ambiguous icons, crowded cards, and free-text configuration when a finite choice exists.

### Design Principles
1. Make common reporting tasks selectable in one guided area with one primary action.
2. Prefer multi-select choices, chips, and explicit states over typed filter values.
3. Reveal only the settings required by the report types the user selected.
4. Keep advanced filters available but visually secondary.
5. Preserve RTL clarity, keyboard access, responsive layouts, and strong selected/unselected contrast.
